With a commitment to quality and usability across our hire range, the Allen & Heath SQ series of digital mixers fitted the bill perfectly.

These consoles have generous local I/O and room for expansion over SLink, taking each desk up to 48 inputs. Other notable features include 36 buses, 96kHz processing, 12 stereo mixes plus LR, eight stereo FX engines and a built-in 32x32 USB audio interface.

We now have both the compact SQ5 and the medium-format SQ6 in stock. Their reasonable cost means we can pass the saving on with favourable hire pricing.
