After a successful Jubilee concert, InIlford's event organisers asked us back for their Christmas concert. As before, the schedule was tight: an early set-up window and hours that ran well into the evening.

## How we worked it

Our Bristol crew set up early in the morning, our London crew ran the show for the day, and all teams packed the show down in the evening in under an hour.

## What we delivered

To get sound travelling up the street we used a long-throw PA: four Martin Audio W8C, four WS218X subwoofers and four LE12J monitors, all on Linea Research amplifiers. With the extended hours we added four Chauvet Maverick MK2 washes to the stage, all on a Stagemobil L stage with wings for the event branding.
