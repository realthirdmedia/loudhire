Misplaced Theatre approached us to provide 4ft x 4ft Litedeck platforms and high-power lighting with colour control for a Shakespeare production at The Mount Without, one of Bristol's most atmospheric venues.

## What we delivered

Our Chauvet LED fixtures gave rich colour mixing without needing a heavy mains supply or a generator, and complemented the venue's in-house lighting well. The company controlled the system themselves for each performance using our ChamSys MQ70 desk.

The run was a huge success, with tickets selling out the day before opening.
