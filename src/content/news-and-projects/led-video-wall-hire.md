We provide LED video wall systems for indoor and outdoor use, either on their own or with the sound, microphones, cameras and lighting that turn a screen into an event.

## The screen

Our video wall is rated for indoor and outdoor use, with a 3.9mm pixel pitch, excellent colour and up to 6,000 nits of brightness. We adjust the brightness to suit the environment, so it works in full daylight and does not glare after dark.

The smallest configuration we usually supply is 4m wide by 2m tall. We can build it straight from level ground, on top of a stage deck, or suspend it from our 6m x 6m black truss structure for larger events. For bigger screens we use a 6m x 6m Tomcat video wall structure with full rear support.

For remote sites we can stream content over a Starlink internet link.

## Live broadcasts

An outdoor-rated wall is a great way to get people out of the house and into an event space without missing prime-time television. This summer we set up a 4m x 2m wall on a stage deck at The Farmer's Dog in Burford for a live viewing of Britain's Got Talent.

## Presentations and content playback

The wall can go out in any size configuration: a standard 16:9 screen, or a specific shape to suit the content you are showing. For keynotes and presentations we add microphones and lighting so the whole room works together. Pictured above is a 4m x 2m wall we set up for the Hawkstone VIP area at FarmFest.

## World Cup football

When the content is the main event, choosing the right screen size matters even more. For the World Cup we supplied a 5.5m x 3m wall at The Farmer's Dog in Oxfordshire, built on a Litedeck structure with full video wall support behind it. With kick-off times varying, we adjusted the brightness before each match to suit daylight or evening conditions.
