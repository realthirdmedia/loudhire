As demand grows for high-quality radio microphones and higher channel counts, we have added the Sennheiser EW-D range of wireless systems to our hire stock, replacing our G3 and G4 models.

## Main features

- Up to 90 channels of clean audio thanks to 56MHz of tuning bandwidth per unit
- iPhone and Android app control, including function locking on the transmitters
- Automatic frequency scanning and channel allocation on set-up
- 134dB of input dynamic range, reducing clipping and the need for sensitivity selection
- Digital transmission between transmitter and receiver
- Racked in 4s and 8s, ready to go
