2023 was an amazing year for Loud Hire. We provided PA, lighting and staging equipment to more than 300 events, making it our busiest year yet. We enjoyed working with clients old and new, and building more business-to-business relationships with venues, agencies and other production companies.

Highlights included our regular events at [Bath Racecourse](/news-and-projects/the-wurzels-bath-racecourse/) and special projects such as [flying a sound system in a hot air balloon](/news-and-projects/eats-everything-hot-air-balloon/).

## Investment

Alongside more productions, 2023 saw our largest reinvestment in technology to date: new [Danley Sound Labs PA systems](/news-and-projects/danley-sound-labs-pa/), Chauvet IP-rated lighting, [Sennheiser wireless systems](/news-and-projects/wireless-microphone-hire/) and touring line and microphone packages.

## Looking ahead

With a large number of repeat customers already booked in, we spent the start of the year designing PA, lighting and staging systems for festivals, theatres and tours, with more new equipment landing in January and February.

If you have not worked with us before, we would be glad to hear about your upcoming projects. [Get in touch](/contact/).
