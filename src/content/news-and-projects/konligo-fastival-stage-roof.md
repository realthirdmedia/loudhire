Finding a small stage that looks good is harder than it should be. Many events end up with a pop-up gazebo or a marquee on a deck. Our new Konligo Fastival roof solves that: it can be delivered and set up straight onto grass or on top of a stage deck, and it takes around 20 minutes to build.

## The structure

- 6m x 4m internal space, 8m x 4m external footprint (excluding deck)
- 200kg roof loading, enough to rig both front and back lighting
- Builds on grass or on a Litedeck structure
- Custom event branding can be added to the back wall

It is the right size for small festivals, private festival-style events, or as a large front-of-house structure for a bigger stage.

## Out on hire with a full production package

The photographs show the roof deployed on a 1ft Litedeck base with a full production package: a d&b audiotechnik C7 PA system, the right companion for a medium-sized crowd, and stage lighting rigged to the roof. Booking sound, lighting and staging together makes the most of crew and transport, and it means one team owns the whole build.

We set the roof up without a back wall to let a breeze through on a hot summer day, and because the Cotswold backdrop was too good to hide. The evening ran a three-piece band followed by a DJ. With an internal standing space of 5.2m x 4m the stage was big enough for the acts without losing the intimate feel for the crowd.
