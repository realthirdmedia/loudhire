Aerospace Bristol, a regular client, asked us to help with their fifth birthday celebrations: an evening of drinks and light dining for VIP guests, marking five years since the museum opened and revealing the new Bristol Fighter exhibition.

## What we delivered

Creating deep blue washes with conventional lighting would normally mean a huge power draw and extra dimming and distribution. Our Chauvet Ovation F-915FC fresnels gave us high output and precise colour mixing while running from the in-floor power outlets around the museum.

Front light came from the F-915FCs. We then added ETC Source Four profiles fitted with breakup gobos, so the wooden structure of the aircraft appeared caught in a night scene. The event was also a good workout for our ChamSys MQ70 console.
