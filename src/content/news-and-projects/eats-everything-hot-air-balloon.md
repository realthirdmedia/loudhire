When DJ Eats Everything planned a set in a hot air balloon to celebrate a new single, Loud Hire made sure the music could be heard from the sky. We supplied the speakers and the power, turning a flight from Bath to Bristol into a sky-high concert. The project was covered by the [BBC](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/uk-england-bristol-66824964).

## The challenge

Filling the streets of Bath with sound from a basket 500ft in the air is not a normal brief. With strict weight limits and no power on board, we had to fit batteries, inverters, amplifiers, CDJs and speakers into the basket while leaving room for the pilot, co-pilot and the DJ.

## What we delivered

The speaker system of choice was our Danley Sound Labs SM80 loudspeakers: lightweight and long-throw, the right combination for the job, paired with a Danley DNA amplifier. The sound was crystal clear at street level, and plenty of people on the ground were left wondering how such a compact system, flying at 500ft, could make so much noise with no distortion.
