We recently added new effects lighting and followspots to our hire stock.

## New LED effects lighting

We took delivery of twelve Elumen8 Tour Batten TWs. Main features include:

- Lower power consumption than a regular sunstrip
- Warm and cold LEDs recreating the classic warm colour trail of a sunstrip
- RDM-enabled control
- Runs on an existing sunstrip show file
- 8-bit or 16-bit dimming

## Followspots

The Canto 1200 MK2 followspots are high-output, compact spots with all the characteristics you would expect of a professional followspot:

- Well-balanced fixture for smooth operation
- Smooth shutter and dimmer, precision iris
- High output with an 8° to 22° beam angle
- Up to 40m throw
