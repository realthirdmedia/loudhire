Hawkstone asked Loud Hire to oversee the event production for a private launch of their new television adverts, held at The Farmer's Dog in the Cotswolds.

## The event

The day was built around live entertainment: several bands, The Wurzels and beatboxers, plus a live performance from The Farmers Choir, each singer on their own radio microphone. A separate stretch tent hosted product tasting. Because the venue is a busy working pub, everything had to be built overnight and be ready without disrupting the day's trade.

## What we delivered

Big Stage Hire provided the 10m x 6m stage. We populated it with a Danley Sound Labs PA system and a deliberately understated lighting design that suited a daytime, outdoor audience. To launch the adverts themselves we supplied a video playback system feeding a 7m x 3m LED video wall rigged above the stage.

The overnight build went to plan and the whole day ran without a hitch. The adverts went on to be banned by the broadcasting regulator, which only added to the story.
