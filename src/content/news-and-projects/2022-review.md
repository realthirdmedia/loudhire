The Loud Hire team worked through the Covid-19 pandemic refreshing our stock to match changes in the events industry. With rapid-deployment PA and lighting systems we can turn events around at very short notice. During the reopening of events in summer 2021 we were regularly quoting for events due to start within one to two weeks.

## Complete packages

To match our sound and lighting offering we teamed up with Big Stage Hire to offer complete sound, lighting, power and staging packages from one team.

## New stock for 2022

For summer 2022 we added new Allen & Heath SQ5 and SQ6 mixers, which pair well with our Martin Audio W8LM, WS218X and LE12J stock for live events and festivals. Fresh from the factory, our Sennheiser EW-D radio microphone systems offer up to 99 channels of clean, intermodulation-free audio. For smaller do-it-yourself parties we also took delivery of a large order of Electro-Voice active point-source speakers.

If you are organising an event and need sound, stage lighting or a complete production package, [contact us](/contact/).
