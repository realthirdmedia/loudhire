Ilford has a thriving market and a pedestrianised high street, and as a Business Improvement District it is always looking for new ways to bring people into town and support local businesses. We were asked to supply a full production package for the Jubilee celebrations, including staging, PA, lighting and power.

## The challenge

A busy London high street never sleeps, with activity around the clock. That left a very short window between arriving on site and the production going live. Using a trailer stage and rapid-deployment sound systems we completed the set-up within two hours, ready for a day of local talent: dancers, singers, a saxophonist and acoustic acts.

## What we delivered

Eight Martin Audio W8LM with four WS218 subwoofers and four LE12J foldback wedges, all driven by Linea Research amplifiers and mixed on an Allen & Heath SQ5. Lighting was our Alu Hex pars, and the stage was a Stagemobil L.
