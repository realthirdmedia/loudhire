Our clients had a very clear vision: a festival-style wedding set against a picturesque backdrop. With several caterers, an on-site bar and toilets, the site also had demanding power requirements to meet. After an initial site visit and detailed discussions we had the green light.

## What we delivered

Inside the rear tipi we built a Litedeck staging system taking up around a third of the space, used by a high-energy band. We lit the stage with LED parcans, Chauvet Maverick MK2 washes using pixel mapping, and LED sunstrips for effects.

Outside, 200m of festoon on dimmers created the right ambient light across the site, with high-power uplighting picking out the trees.
