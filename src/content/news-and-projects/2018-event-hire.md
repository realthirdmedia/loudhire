We are quickly booking up for the summer of 2018 and look forward to supporting clients both new and old.

## Our goals

We are well established in PA hire and event lighting, fulfilling event requirements across the south of England. As well as continuing to grow our coverage nationwide, we want to strengthen our local client base and help bring the running costs of local events under control, because cost so often stops live events from happening.

## Our equipment

With investments such as [line array technology](/news-and-projects/martin-audio-line-array/) and [Hex par cans](/news-and-projects/elumen8-alu-hex-par-64/) we have built a hire stock that is versatile across the seasons: just as useful on summer festivals as on winter corporate events.

## Why us

We believe planning and customer support matter as much as the technical delivery on the day. Every event is designed in CAD and written up in our office, so all of our staff, full-time and freelance, know their role on site. Our equipment is tested in our warehouse before every event; nothing turns up fresh off another job without being checked and prepped.
