We were asked to provide a video display system inside a stretch tent to show video content and presentation slides during a team meeting.

## The challenge

Stretch tents let in a lot of daylight. Projectors and screens were not an option, because the picture would have been washed out.

## What we delivered

We deployed our 3.9mm pixel pitch outdoor modular video wall at 4m wide by 2m tall, controlled with a Novastar processor. Running at half brightness it was still clearly visible across the tent.

To make sure the presenters were heard cleanly we paired the wall with a Danley PA system and a Sennheiser wireless microphone. Site power was limited, so we also brought one of our 60kVA road-tow generators.
