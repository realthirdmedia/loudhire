We supplied the sound equipment for a private party where Gorgon City played a set mixing deep house, disco and funk.

## What we delivered

A pair of Pioneer CDJ-3000 players with a DJM-900NXS2 mixer for the DJ, a Shure SM58 for announcements, and a complete Electro-Voice ETX front-of-house system made up of two ETX-12P loudspeakers and two ETX-18SP subwoofers.
