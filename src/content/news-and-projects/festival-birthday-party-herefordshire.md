A farmer near Hereford contacted us wanting to clear the cattle off his land and host an extra special joint birthday party for his sons. "A night to remember" was the assignment.

## Planning

During a site visit we helped the client with a site layout plan and worked out the power requirements with his suppliers. Big Stage Hire provided their 7m x 6m L stage.

## On the night

On stage we deployed our Martin Audio W8LM sound system with WS218X subwoofers, and lit the show with LED Par 64s, Chauvet Maverick MK2 washes and sunstrips. Three bands were mixed by our engineers on an Allen & Heath SQ6 console before a DJ closed the night.
